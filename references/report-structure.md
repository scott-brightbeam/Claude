# Capstone Evaluation Report Structure

## Document Specifications

### Page Setup
- **Size:** A4 (210mm × 297mm)
- **Margins:** 1 inch (25.4mm) all sides
- **Font:** Calibri throughout
- **Brand Color:** #F47920 (Orange)

### Header
- Right-aligned: "[Participant Name] - Capstone Evaluation"
- Font: Calibri 9pt, Gray (#999999)

### Footer
- Center-aligned: "Prepared with AI assistance and reviewed by Ibec staff"
- Font: Calibri 9pt, Italic, Gray (#666666)

---

## Table Styling

**Header Rows:**
- Background: #F47920 (Orange)
- Text: White, Bold
- Alignment: Center

**Data Rows:**
- Background: White
- Text: Black
- Borders: Light gray (#CCCCCC), thin

**Score Cells:**
- Color-coded by band:
  - EXCEPTIONAL: #2E7D32 (Dark Green)
  - STRONG: #4CAF50 (Light Green)
  - DEVELOPING: #FFC107 (Yellow)
  - BASIC: #FF9800 (Orange)
  - INSUFFICIENT: #F44336 (Red)
